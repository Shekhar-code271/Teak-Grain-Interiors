About Us

We started as a small carpentry workshop and grew into a full interior design studio. Because we build everything ourselves, we control quality, timelines and cost.

Our team of designers, carpenters and site supervisors works with you from first sketch to final handover.

Our process

  • Free consultation and site visit
  • Design and 3D preview
  • Flat-rate quotation
  • In-house manufacturing
  • Installation and handover

Frequently asked questions

How long does a full-home project take?

Most 2–3 BHK homes finish in 45–60 days after design approval.

Is the pricing really fixed?

Yes. Each package is flat-rate, and changes are quoted in writing first.

Do you offer a warranty?

Every package includes a warranty, up to 5 years on the Luxe plan.

Can I see a 3D design before I commit?

Yes. Signature and Luxe include a full 3D preview with revisions.
